About this resource

Family Patterns Lab offers accessible psychoeducation, guided reflection, practical boundary tools, and relationship-focused exercises for people navigating difficult family dynamics. Use what fits your circumstances and move at a pace that feels manageable.

A gentle safety note

Boundaries and distance can affect safety in abusive or coercive situations. Consider individualized support and a safety plan. In the U.S., call or text 988 for a mental-health crisis; call 911 for immediate danger. This website does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.
